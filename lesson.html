Common Greetings

In English-speaking cultures, greetings change depending on who you are talking to and the time of day. For formal situations, we use "Good morning" or "Good afternoon." However, for friends, we often use more casual expressions.

"Hey! How's it going?" or "What's up?" are common ways to start a conversation with someone you already know well.

Practice these phrases with a partner to improve your natural flow. Remember, intonation is key to sounding natural!
